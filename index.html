This is a camera artwork about how a familiar scene can become unfamiliar. Each preset is a different visual language: displaced images, accumulated frames, unstable edges, bending geometry, or a narrowing field.

Start with the generated night scene, upload a photo, or enable your camera. Filters affect the whole image, including you. Use Show original to compare, turn off animation to freeze the motion, or hit Back to sober to remove the filter.

The preset names are artistic references. Experiences vary, and these effects cannot show what a substance actually feels like or diagnose intoxication. Everything runs in your browser.
